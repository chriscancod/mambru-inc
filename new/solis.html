01 · Hardware

Ours. Down to the board.

Built around the Rockchip RK3588, eight cores and an onboard NPU, chip class usually found in flagship tablets rather than phones.

RK35888-core CPU and NPU, with its own power chip and three 6 A converters.
8 GB memoryOn the first board. 16 GB is a later target and costs about twice as much memory.
128 GB storageeMMC 5.1. Chosen because real suppliers have it in stock.
Custom board6-layer design target, 147 × 77 mm. Layout in progress.

02 · Power

A battery that tops itself up.

A thin-film solar band under the rear shell feeds the battery. By our own math it adds roughly 0.6 to 0.9 W in good light. It refills the battery between charges. It cannot run the phone by itself.

Solar top-up bandThrough an MPPT controller.
Magnetic chargingQi2-style wireless charging, so there is no port to wear out. Whether there is also a wired port is still being decided.
Removable batteryA tool-free tray, 5,200 mAh target. No adhesive.

03 · Software

Veynor OS.

Our own software, written from scratch. It runs in a web browser on a phone or tablet today. The phone build has not started.

Veynor PayPayment requests between two Veynor accounts. No bank or card is connected and no money moves. Tap-to-pay is future work.

Why not just buy a flagship?

Sealed shut vs. built to open.

Typical flagship

  • Battery glued in
  • Memory and storage soldered down
  • The charging port wears out
  • Waste heat is only a problem

Veynor Solis

  • Battery in a tool-free tray
  • Modular board (planned)
  • Magnetic wireless charging
  • A solar band that tops up the battery

The modular board is a plan and is not proven until a second revision fits the first shell.
