The device

One button. One switch.
One small screen.

A 128 × 64 screen, a single button and a slide switch between Chat and Pet. That is the whole interface.

The screen above is a real frame from our simulator. Product photos will come when the hardware is built.

How it works

Hold. Ask. Read.

Hold the buttonThe red light comes on and the charm starts listening.
Your iPhone thinksThe charm sends your voice over Bluetooth. The phone turns it into words with Apple's on-device speech recognition, so your voice stays on your phone.
Read the answerEasy questions are answered on the phone. The answer appears on the charm a page at a time. Tap to see the next page.

Design

Small on purpose.

The charm is the ears and the face. Your iPhone is the brain and the memory.

The charm

  • Reads the button and the switch
  • Listens and sends sound
  • Draws pictures on the screen
  • Stores nothing important

Your iPhone

  • Understands the question
  • Keeps the pet, the history and waiting questions
  • Answers easy questions itself
  • Swap the charm's batteries and nothing is lost

Can I buy it?

Not yet. There is no price and no ship date. Joining the waitlist is only an email address and nothing is charged.

Does it need my iPhone?

Yes. The charm is built to work with an iPhone, which does the thinking. The iPhone app is still being built.

Where does my voice go?

The design keeps it on your phone: speech is recognised on-device with Apple's speech recognition. Only harder questions would ever leave the phone, and only as text. That part is not built yet.

Is it finished?

No. The software is built and tested on a computer. The first tests on real hardware are only starting.
